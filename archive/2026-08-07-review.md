## Today's 3 picks — all completed

### 1. JDLA decision
Reviewed all 4 uploaded reference docs (定款, 会員規程, 入会案内,
入会申込書). Found the 定款/会員規程 (the actual governing documents, both
revised 2024) only define two voting "member" (社員) categories: 正会員
(companies whose *core business* is deep learning — e.g. ABEJA, Preferred
Networks; ¥300k entry + ¥600k/yr) and 有識者会員 (individual DL
researchers; free; needs 2+ combined recommendations from existing
正会員/有識者会員/理事, where a director's recommendation counts too, not
just existing members). 賛助会員 (the tier JDLA's reply email mentioned)
does exist per the 入会案内 guide, but isn't a voting member — it's a
corporate-sponsor observer tier at ¥1,000,000-5,000,000/year (Silver/
Gold/Platinum), no voting rights, populated by large corporates (Deloitte,
KPMG, SMBC, JAL, SoftBank, etc.).

Decision: 正会員 ruled out (BARTOSS K.K.'s core business isn't deep
learning itself), 賛助会員 ruled out (poor cost/value for a solo
consultancy), pursue 有識者会員 via the interview JDLA offered. Rather
than decline outright over the Japanese-interview requirement (user has
no Japanese-speaking support at BARTOSS K.K. currently, and his own
Japanese isn't yet strong enough for a live interview), drafted and sent
a Japanese-language reply asking if the interview could be conducted in
English, with a fallback of revisiting once Japanese support is in place.
Awaiting JDLA's reply.
